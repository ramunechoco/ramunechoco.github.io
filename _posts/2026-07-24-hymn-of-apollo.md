**Apóllôn, Incense: mánna.**[^1]

Come blessed healer, slayer of Tityós, the Shining One of Parnassós,<br>
Memphian, renowned, exclaimed with cries of joy, bestower of bliss,<br>
Bearing a golden lyre, generative, agrarian, Pythian, Titan,<br>
Gryneian, Sminthéfs, slayer of the Pýthohn, Dælphic, Oracular one,<br>
Wild, radiant divinity, lovely one, glorious son,<br>
Leader of the Mousai, who creates joy, whose arrows achieve their aim, mighty archer,<br>
Vrángkheian and Didymæan, you who achieve from afar, prophet, holy one,<br>
Delian king who has eyes shining light on mortals,<br>
Golden-haired, you who bring to light clear oracular utterances,<br>
Hear me with gracious soul as I pray on behalf of mankind,<br>
For you gaze upon all the boundless Aithír,<br>
And upon the blessed earth and from above through the night<br>
In the peace of the night with its starry-eyed darkness<br>
You cast light upon the roots below and hold the boundaries of the whole kózmos:<br>
For the origin and completion are both in your care,<br>
The cause of the blooming of all things; with your resonant lyre you harmonize the entire axis of the heavens,<br>
At one time advancing to the lowest pitch,<br>
And at another to the highest, at times in the Dorian mode,<br>
You temper the entire axis; you keep the tribes of living creatures distinct,<br>
You have mingled in harmony the share of all mortal men,<br>
You have mixed an equal measure of winter and summer,<br>
You have arranged the highest three strings in the winter, the lowest in the summer,<br>
You are the seasonal Dorian flower of the lovely spring,<br>
Thereupon the mortals celebrate and call you lord and<br>
Pan, the two-horned God who sends the whistling winds,<br>
Wherefore you bear the formative seal of the entire Kózmos.<br>
Hear, happy one, my supplicating voice, thus saving the initiates!

[^1]: English translation not mine, taken entirely from [hellenicgods.org](https://www.hellenicgods.org/the-orphic-hymn-to-apollo)
